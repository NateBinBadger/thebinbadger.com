A Neighborhood Business With A Neighborhood Mission

We didn't build The Bin Badger to compete with national franchises. We built it because we live here, and we saw a way to make this street — and the ones around it — a little better.

One free neighbor per month. Every block that hits its milestone unlocks permanent free service for a senior or disabled neighbor who can't move their trash cans alone.

Nominate a neighbor. Know someone on your street who struggles on trash day? Tell us. We'll prioritize them as your block grows.

Connected to Big Brothers Big Sisters. Our community give-back is tied to our growth. The more of Wilton Manors we serve, the more families we can support through the program.

Wilton Manors code compliance experts. We know every city ordinance — trash cans within 5 ft of the curb, wheels facing the house, invisible from the street by end of day. Every time.
